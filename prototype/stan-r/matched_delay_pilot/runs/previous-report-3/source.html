# Latency-aware control report

Results are simulation measurements, not hardware claims. The 500 ns line is a
hypothesis marker. Delay-aware policies can compensate for predictable delays;
performance need not collapse or be monotonic. No collapse is forced by the code.

Error bars show one standard error across episode seeds, not independent training
replicates. Train multiple seeds to study optimization variability. Settling time
excludes unsuccessful episodes; read it together with settled/loss fractions.
Pre-kick losses have missing post-kick errors and must not be interpreted as good
tracking. The oscillator parameters are illustrative, not machine calibrated.

Host benchmark p99 is recorded separately from assumed compute budgets. It does
not establish GPU, FPGA or custom-hardware latency. Full end-to-end timing requires
sensor, transport, compute, DAC and beam-flight measurements.
